Message on JKBOSE Results

Class X & XII Session 2025

On the announcement of the JKBOSE Class X and XII results, I extend my sincere congratulations to all those who have been part of this academic journey and contributed to the outcomes achieved this year.

To the Heads of Schools I wish to particularly acknowledge your leadership and responsibility in steering institutions with vision and stability. Your role in maintaining academic standards, supporting faculty, and ensuring a healthy learning environment forms the foundation upon which educational success is built.
To Our Dedicated Teachers My appreciation goes to you, whose dedication, patience, and daily engagement with students transform curricula into meaningful learning. Your quiet perseverance and commitment continue to shape young minds and inspire confidence far beyond the classroom.
To the Students These results mark an important milestone. For those who achieved their goals, may this success encourage humility. For those who faced setbacks, remember that growth is not defined by one examination; resilience and consistent effort will always open new paths forward.
To the Parents I recognize your indispensable role. Your trust, encouragement, and sacrifices provide students with strength and stability. Your partnership with schools remains vital to a child’s overall development.
Our Commitment The Private Schools Association Jammu and Kashmir (PSAJK) remains committed to working collectively with school leadership, educators, parents, and students to strengthen quality education, ethical practices, and holistic development across Jammu & Kashmir.

I extend my best wishes to all students for their future endeavours and to the entire education community for continued progress and success.

Faisal Islam Mir
General Secretary
Private Schools Association Jammu and Kashmir (PSAJK)
